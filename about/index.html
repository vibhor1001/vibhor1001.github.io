About PropertyFlow

The platform for UK short-term let hosts.

PropertyFlow brings channel management, AI Cognito Pricing, guest messaging, turnover tasks, document reminders and financials into one dashboard. Built and proven in Edinburgh, one of the UK’s toughest licensing markets. Now live across the UK.

Our mission

The UK short-term let market is being reshaped by regulation. Mandatory licensing in Scotland, registration schemes coming to England and Wales, and tourist taxes set council by council are raising the bar for every host. The hosts who keep their paperwork in order are the ones who keep letting.

We built PropertyFlow so hosts of any size can run a well-documented business from a single dashboard, without stitching together a channel manager, a pricing tool, a document tracker and a spreadsheet of payouts. Two ways to use the platform (your channels or PropertyFlow channels) and one way to build a business with us (the Partner Programme).

What we stand for

Principles that run the platform

Compliance first

Being legal is a competitive advantage. Licences and certificates are stored with renewal reminders on every plan. Applications and renewals are handled for Partner properties.

Simple pricing

No setup fees. No lock-in. Channels, messaging and document reminders on every plan; AI Cognito Pricing on Premium.

Start free, scale simply

Free for up to 3 properties on your own channels. Premium is £9.99 per property per month plus VAT, on every property including the first three, and unlocks AI Cognito Pricing and unlimited properties. Want more bookings? Switch to PropertyFlow channels. Want to introduce landlords and earn on every property? Apply to the Partner Programme.

Hosts in control

Your listings, your accounts, your data. Connect your own channels and disconnect them whenever you like. Switch between your channels and PropertyFlow channels at any time.

All prices exclude VAT. VAT is added at the standard rate once our registration is confirmed.
