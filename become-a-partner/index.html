08Questions

Partner Programme questions

More detail: How Partners earn · What stays yours · What we take on · What we look for · Onboarding

Who contracts with the landlord?

PropertyFlow. Every landlord signs PropertyFlow's standard Landlord Agreement, and PropertyFlow holds the short-term let licence or registration for the property.

What do I earn?

50% of PropertyFlow's management fee, excluding VAT, on every property you introduce, paid monthly on a self-billed statement. Own properties on standard terms, no commission. No commission on landlords PropertyFlow contracts directly. PropertyFlow never charges you a fee.

Who handles guest messages?

The back office, on the AI messenger, from first enquiry to check-out. Your Portfolio Manager loops you in on escalations.

Who is my contact at PropertyFlow?

Your named Portfolio Manager in the back office. They are your day-to-day contact for every property you introduce.

Can I put my own properties on the programme?

Yes, on standard landlord terms. No commission is paid on your own properties.

What insurance is in place, and what happens if a guest damages a property?

PropertyFlow holds Public Liability, Employer's Liability and Professional Indemnity insurance. Guest damage is handled under each booking channel's own damage protection. On Partner properties your Portfolio Manager files and manages the claim. On your own connected channels you claim directly with the channel. On PropertyFlow-channel bookings we pursue it for you.

Where is the programme open?

Expanding across the UK. Actively reviewing Partner applications in the UK and Dubai, with Europe to follow.

What happens after I apply?

We reply within two working days. If it looks like a fit we book a call. Accepted applicants sign the agreement electronically, receive the Partner Programme Guide, complete training and introduce their first landlord. The Guide covers the detail, including how leaving the programme works.
