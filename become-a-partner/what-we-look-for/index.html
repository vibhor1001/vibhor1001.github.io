Applying

Who we accept.

By application. We accept around one in five. Here is what we look for, so you can tell quickly if it is a fit.

Landlords you can introduce

A network of property owners, or a credible plan to build one.

Properties guests will want

Well-kept places in areas with genuine short-let demand.

Time for relationships

You want to spend your week winning landlords and looking after them, not running changeovers.

Comfortable with the structure

PropertyFlow is the contracting party and licence holder for every property. You trade under your own name and keep the relationship.

Not sure if you qualify? Apply anyway. We reply within two working days and we will be straight with you about whether it is a fit.
