About PropertyFlow

What we do

PropertyFlow is the platform for short-term let hosts. Hosts connect their own Airbnb and Booking.com accounts (we call this Your channels), or we list their properties on the channels they are not on (PropertyFlow channels). The PropertyFlow Partner Programme lets local property professionals introduce landlords and earn 50% of our management fee, excluding VAT, on every property they bring in. Built and proven in Edinburgh, one of the UK’s toughest licensing markets. Now live across the UK.
