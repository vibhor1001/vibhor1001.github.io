## What Does a Property Management Partner Actually Do?

**The Partner role is centred on relationships and growth.**

You find property owners who could benefit from short-term-let management. You speak to them, explain the service, help them understand what is involved and bring suitable properties into the portfolio.

You then remain their local relationship contact.

PropertyFlow's model allows you to trade under your own name and keep that relationship. The landlord contracts with PropertyFlow, while your role remains focused on the relationship and growing the business. A named Portfolio Manager supports the day-to-day operation behind the scenes.
