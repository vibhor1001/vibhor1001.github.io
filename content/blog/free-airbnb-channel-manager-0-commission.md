## Compliance, Damage and Insurance: What Is and Is Not Covered

Free software should be clear about its limits, so here they are.

**Compliance.** Short-term let rules differ by nation and council. **PropertyFlow gives you licence and certificate reminders and document storage for each property, with tourist tax configured per council.** It does not give legal advice, so confirm what applies to you with your local authority. Applications and renewals are handled for Partner properties only.

**Guest damage.** Damage is handled under each booking channel's own protection. On your own connected channels, you claim directly with the channel. On PropertyFlow-channel bookings, PropertyFlow pursues the claim for you.

**Insurance.** PropertyFlow holds Public Liability, Employer's Liability and Professional Indemnity insurance. You should keep your own buildings and contents insurance in place.

**Guest vetting.** Identity and payment verification is handled by each booking channel. PropertyFlow gives you every guest's details and messages in one place.
