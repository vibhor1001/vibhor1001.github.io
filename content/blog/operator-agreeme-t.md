**PropertyFlow Partner Programme: Standard Form v1.2**

*PropertyFlow Management Partners Ltd and the Operator named in the Particulars. 6 September 2026. Square brackets mark the Operator's details, completed in the Particulars for each Operator; nothing else in this document is left open. The programme is marketed as the PropertyFlow Partner Programme and the Operator as a PropertyFlow Verified Partner; in this Agreement the defined term is "Operator".*

**How this Agreement works.** PropertyFlow Management Partners Ltd ("PropertyFlow") is the property manager: it contracts with every Landlord, holds every Licence and every pound of client money, and runs the properties remotely with its Service Partners and Maintenance Partners. The Operator is PropertyFlow's local sales channel: it introduces Landlords, closes them on PropertyFlow's standard terms from the Platform, in the meeting, and holds the relationship. For that the Operator receives 50% of PropertyFlow's management fee, excluding VAT, for as long as the Landlord stays and the Operator remains in the programme. The Operator never handles money, never contracts with a Landlord and never does the physical work. That is the whole design, and it protects the Operator as much as PropertyFlow. The Partner Programme Guide explains the reasons in plain English and answers the questions Partners ask.

## Structural Terms

The following principles are fundamental to this Agreement and to PropertyFlow's position with every Landlord, licensing authority and tax filing. A breach of any of them by the Operator is a material breach that cannot be remedied (clause 15.3(a)).

- 1. **Agent, never principal.** PropertyFlow acts solely as agent of each Landlord in marketing, letting and managing an Introduced Property. The contract for the supply of accommodation is between the Landlord and the Guest. The Operator acts as agent for no one under this Agreement and supplies no accommodation.
    2. **One contracting party.** PropertyFlow (or the Group company named in the Landlord Agreement) is the sole contracting party with each Landlord. Every Introduced Property is managed under a Landlord Agreement in PropertyFlow's form. No Landlord Agreement, and no right under one, transfers to the Operator in any circumstances.
    3. **Client Money.** All Client Money is received and held only in the Client Account. Neither the Operator nor any Operator Principal shall receive, hold, control, direct or give payment instructions in respect of Client Money: not temporarily, not in transition, not for convenience, and not as agent for any Landlord. No account or Booking Channel payout method of the Operator or an Operator Principal may be used for any Introduced Property.
    4. **Booking Channels.** All Booking Channel accounts, listings, payout settings and tax information for Introduced Properties are held and controlled by PropertyFlow. Where a Booking Channel requires a named host, that is PropertyFlow or the Landlord; where a Landlord's existing listing is kept on the Landlord's own account, PropertyFlow is the manager and sole payee. Any existing listing held by the Operator for a property that becomes an Introduced Property is migrated under Schedule 4 Part C.
    5. **Licences.** PropertyFlow is the applicant for and holder of each Licence (or, where the authority requires the Landlord to hold it, the named agent on it). The named day-to-day manager on any Licence is Group personnel, never the Operator. No Introduced Property is marketed or hosted without a Licence in force for the relevant dates.
    6. **The Landlord is PropertyFlow's client.** The Operator introduces Landlords and holds the relationship on PropertyFlow's behalf. The Operator's only asset under this Agreement is its Book (clause 15). It acquires no goodwill in, and no other interest in, any Landlord, property, Landlord Agreement, Licence, listing, review or data.

## Commission

- 1. In consideration of the Operator's services, PropertyFlow shall pay the Operator Commission of 50% of the Fee Received for each calendar month in respect of each Introduced Property, for so long as the Landlord Agreement remains in force and this Agreement continues.
    2. **Calculation and fee base.** The Management Fee is charged on Net Revenue: Gross Revenue for bookings checking out in the month, less Booking Channel commission at the actual

amount the channel deducted, payment processing fees and Guest refunds, and before Tourist Tax is deducted. Tourist Tax, the Landlord's property costs and any other deduction are taken from the Client Ledger after the Management Fee. Where PropertyFlow is VAT-registered, VAT is added to the Management Fee on the Landlord's Statement and accounted for to HMRC; it forms no part of the Fee Received and no part of Commission. The Fee Received is the Management Fee, excluding VAT, actually deducted and received in cleared funds. Commission is therefore 10% of Net Revenue on a property at the standard 20% rate and 7.5% at the 15% floor, before and after VAT registration alike. Schedule 2 shows the working. Where a Landlord Agreement provides for a different rate or base, the Management Fee is calculated as that agreement provides and Commission remains 50% of the Fee Received.

- 1. **Cleaning fees.** Any cleaning fee charged to a Guest forms part of Gross Revenue and is not a pass-through of the Service Partner's charge. PropertyFlow sets the guest cleaning fee as part of pricing, and the actual Service Partner charge for each clean is a Landlord cost paid from the Client Ledger under clause 7.4. Commission is calculated on the Management Fee only and is unaffected by the Service Partner's charge.
    2. **Exclusions.** Commission does not extend to: (a) Client Money or booking revenue; (b) Landlord costs, recharges or supplier charges; (c) any Tourist Tax, VAT, deposit, pre-authorisation or damage recovery; (d) any Platform subscription, channel fee or other Group revenue outside the Management Fee; (e) Landlords PropertyFlow contracts directly or another operator introduces; (f) any property owned by the Operator, an Operator Principal or a Connected Person, which pays the standard Management Fee (or enterprise pricing agreed in the Landlord Agreement) and carries no Commission; or (g) any period after this Agreement ends.
    3. **Clawback.** Where a Management Fee on which Commission has been paid is later refunded, credited, charged back, reversed or written off, the corresponding Commission is deducted from the next Statement or, if none is due, repaid within 30 days of demand.
    4. **Statement and payment.** Within the first five Business Days of each month, once the previous month has been reconciled, PropertyFlow issues the Operator a Statement for that month showing, per Introduced Property: Gross Revenue, Booking Channel commission and payment fees, refunds, Net Revenue, the Management Fee rate and amount, VAT charged on it (if any), Fee Received, Commission and adjustments. Under Schedule 6 PropertyFlow raises a self-billed invoice for the amount shown and pays it on the same day as the Landlord payouts, and in any event by the fifth Business Day, from its own funds and never from Client Money. Commission is exclusive of VAT; if the Operator is VAT-registered, VAT is added on the self-billed invoice and PropertyFlow recovers it. Statements to Landlords and to the Operator are issued by PropertyFlow in its own name and identify PropertyFlow as the property manager and the Operator as the Landlord's contact.
    5. **Queries and set-off.** The Operator raises any query within 30 days of a Statement; undisputed sums are paid on time; disputes go to clause 17, with reasonable access to the supporting reconciliation. PropertyFlow may set off against Commission any sum due from the Operator under this Agreement, with a statement of the basis.
    6. **No other remuneration.** The Operator bears its own costs. No expense is reimbursed unless approved by PropertyFlow in writing in advance. Commission stops on the last day of the Term; nothing is payable for or after termination except Commission accrued to that day (clause 15.9).
