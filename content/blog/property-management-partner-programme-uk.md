## What Is the Property Management Partner Programme?

**PropertyFlow serves two types of property owners: those who want to manage their own properties using free software, and those who want a fully managed service.** The Partner Programme focuses on the second group. As a Partner, you introduce landlords who want operational support, while PropertyFlow runs the service under its management arrangement.

**The basic model is straightforward:**

1. Apply to become a Partner.
2. Find and introduce landlords.
3. PropertyFlow contracts with the landlord and manages the operation.
4. You keep the landlord relationship and continue growing your portfolio.
5. You earn 50% of PropertyFlow's management fee, excluding VAT, on properties you introduce.

## Is This a Property Management Franchise or Network?

**If you are researching how to join a property management franchise or network in the UK, it is worth understanding what the PropertyFlow model actually is.**

It is not a traditional franchise. You trade under your own name and keep the relationship with your landlord. **[PropertyFlow](https://www.propertyflow.uk/)** **is the contracting party and licence holder for managed properties, with each landlord contracting with PropertyFlow under its standard Landlord Agreement.**

That means your role stays focused on the local side of the business: finding property owners, building trust, bringing suitable properties into the portfolio and maintaining those relationships.

PropertyFlow provides the operational structure underneath.

## What Does a Property Management Partner Actually Do?

The Partner role is centred on relationships and portfolio growth.

You find property owners who could benefit from short-term-let management. You speak to them, explain the service and help bring suitable properties into the portfolio.

**Once a property comes on board, you remain the relationship contact.** You are not expected to personally handle every guest message, organise every cleaner or manage every operational issue. PropertyFlow's model puts those responsibilities within its platform, back office and operational network.

Your focus is the part of the business that depends on local knowledge and relationships:

* Finding landlords
* Building trust
* Introducing suitable properties
* Maintaining landlord relationships
* Growing your portfolio

The more suitable properties you bring into the business, the more your management portfolio can grow.

## How Does the Partnership Work Day to Day?

Imagine you have a landlord with two short-term-let properties. You build the relationship, discuss their plans and introduce the properties to PropertyFlow.

**PropertyFlow then handles the operational side.**

The properties are listed and priced. Guests receive messages. Cleaning and changeovers are coordinated. Linen and maintenance are handled through the operational network. Licence applications and renewals are managed where required.

You remain involved when the landlord needs you or when something needs escalation. Then you go back to building the business. You find the next landlord. That landlord may have one property, or several. One relationship can become a larger portfolio, and further introductions can follow.

**The Partner model is therefore built around building a management portfolio, not making a one-off referral.**

## How Do Partners Earn?

**Partners earn 50% of PropertyFlow's management fee, excluding VAT, on every property they introduce.**

Payments are made monthly through a self-billed statement. The management fee for each property is agreed with the landlord and set out in the Landlord Agreement.

The model connects your income to the portfolio you build.

You bring the landlord. You keep the relationship. PropertyFlow runs the operation. You earn from the properties you introduce.
