## How Full Property Management Works

Full property management means handing the day to day running of your short term let to a management company and its team. **PropertyFlow combines connected** **[property management software](https://www.propertyflow.uk/)** **with a fully managed service for owners who want to hand over the work.**

**With fully managed hosting:**

* A local PropertyFlow Verified Partner remains your relationship contact.
* A named Portfolio Manager oversees the property's day to day operation.
* The operations team coordinates listings, bookings, pricing and guest communication.
* Vetted local providers coordinate cleaning, linen, changeovers and maintenance.
* PropertyFlow applies for and holds the relevant short term let licence or registration where required for managed properties.
* You receive monthly payouts and an itemised statement of bookings, deductions and applicable tourist tax.

The management fee is tailored to your property and agreed before you sign. Cleaning and maintenance are charged at cost, without a PropertyFlow mark up.

If you prefer to manage the property yourself, you can use **PropertyFlow's software free for life.**

## Should You Choose Full Management or Manage the Property Yourself?

**The right option depends on how much of the work you want to handle yourself.**

If you enjoy hosting, live near the property and have time to coordinate bookings, guests and cleaners, PropertyFlow's free software lets you manage your own operation.

If you have a full time job, live elsewhere or own several properties, fully managed hosting may suit you better. A local Partner and the operations team coordinate the work while you stay informed about your property.

Consider a landlord with a full time job who owns a holiday cottage in the Lake District. During a busy summer weekend, a guest may need help with check in while a cleaner reports a maintenance issue. **The landlord must coordinate both while managing their other commitments. With full management, the operational team coordinates those tasks.**
