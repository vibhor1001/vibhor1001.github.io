Cookie Policy

Effective from 10 September 2026. Updated 29 September 2026 (Meta Pixel now active; LinkedIn and Google Ads added). Version 1.1.

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how PropertyFlow Technologies Ltd uses cookies and similar technologies (pixels, local storage and software development kits in the apps) on propertyflow.uk, app.propertyflow.uk and the PropertyFlow apps, and how you can control them. It should be read with our Privacy Notice.

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Some are needed for the site to work; others tell us how the site is used or measure advertising. Pixels and software development kits do a similar job on web pages and in apps.

3. Consent

When you first visit the website we show a banner. Strictly necessary cookies are set without consent because the site cannot work without them. Analytics and marketing cookies are set only if you choose to accept them. You can change your choice at any time from the 'Cookie settings' link in the footer of every page. In the apps, analytics can be switched off in Settings.

Google, HubSpot, LinkedIn and Meta set their cookies under their own privacy policies. We use them only in the ways described above and only with your consent for analytics and marketing.

If you reject or withdraw consent, we delete the cookies set on propertyflow.uk. Cookies that LinkedIn and Google set on their own websites (linkedin.com, doubleclick.net) cannot be removed by our site; you can delete them in your browser settings.

6. Managing cookies in your browser

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, parts of the platform will not work.
