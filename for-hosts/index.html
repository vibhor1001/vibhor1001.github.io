Compliance

Know what applies to your property, and when it is due.

Short-term let rules differ by nation and council. PropertyFlow tracks five regimes: Scotland, England, London, Wales and Northern Ireland (Tourism NI certification).

Licensing

Scotland
Every short-term let needs a licence from its council. Keep the licence, certificates and renewal dates for each property in one place.

Planned

England
A national registration scheme is planned. No national licence yet; local rules apply, including London's 90-night limit.

Oct 2026

Wales
Registration opens October 2026. Register by 31 March 2027.

What PropertyFlow does
•Licence and certificate reminders, set once per document
•Document storage for each property
•Tourist tax configured per council
•Applications and renewals handled for Partner properties

Rules vary by council and we do not give legal advice. Confirm what applies with your local authority.

Insurance and guest damage

What is covered, and who handles it.

Insurance

PropertyFlow holds Public Liability, Employer's Liability and Professional Indemnity insurance. Keep your own buildings and contents insurance in place.

Guest damage

Guest damage is handled under each booking channel's own damage protection. On Partner properties your Portfolio Manager files and manages the claim. On your own connected channels you claim directly with the channel. On PropertyFlow-channel bookings we pursue it for you.

Host FAQs

What do I get on Free?

Free on your own Airbnb and Booking.com accounts, with as many properties as you want: one inbox, one calendar, AI messaging, automated guest messages, turnover tasks, document reminders and reporting. No card required. No commission on bookings from your own accounts.

Do I pay per property?

No. Every property on your own Airbnb and Booking.com accounts is free. There is no per-property charge: AI Cognito Pricing is free on every property.

What are PropertyFlow channels and what do they cost?

We list your property on the channels you are not on, and those bookings land in the same inbox and calendar as everything else. The fee is a 5% distribution fee plus VAT of each booking we bring, on top of that channel's own commission at cost. The same rate applies to a Booking.com listing we create for you if you do not have one. Bookings through your own Airbnb or Booking.com account: 0%. Switch PropertyFlow channels off whenever you like. Your own Airbnb and Booking.com accounts keep running as they are.

Does PropertyFlow vet guests?

Guest identity and payment verification is handled by each booking channel; PropertyFlow gives you every guest's details and messages in one place, so you can see who is arriving and what they have asked before they get there.

What happens if a guest damages my property?

Guest damage is handled under each booking channel's own damage protection. On Partner properties your Portfolio Manager files and manages the claim. On your own connected channels you claim directly with the channel. On PropertyFlow-channel bookings we pursue it for you. PropertyFlow holds Public Liability, Employer's Liability and Professional Indemnity insurance. Keep your own buildings and contents insurance in place.

Does PropertyFlow help with licensing and compliance?

Every host gets licence and certificate reminders and document storage for each property, with tourist tax configured per council. Applications and renewals are handled for Partner properties. Rules differ by nation and council and we do not give legal advice, so confirm what applies with your local authority.
