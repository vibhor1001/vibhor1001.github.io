06Everything you need to know

Frequently asked questions

Anything else? Email us at info@propertyflow.uk

What’s included in PropertyFlow’s free plan, and how does pricing work?

Free for up to 3 properties on your own channels: connect your Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo accounts, one inbox, one calendar, AI messaging, reminders. AI Cognito Pricing is on Premium.

What happens when I add more than 3 properties?

A fourth property moves your account to Premium: £9.99 per property per month plus VAT on every property, so four properties is £39.96 plus VAT, with AI Cognito Pricing included.

What can I manage through PropertyFlow?

PropertyFlow brings your bookings, pricing, guest communication, team operations, reporting and compliance together in one place.

Does PropertyFlow help with compliance?

Yes. Every host gets licence and certificate reminders and document storage: upload each document once, set its renewal date and get reminded in time. On Partner properties, applications and renewals are handled for you.

What is the Partner Programme and how does it work?

The PropertyFlow Partner Programme is for people who want to build a management business with us. You introduce landlords and hold the relationship. PropertyFlow contracts with each landlord, holds the licence and runs operations through the back office and vetted Service Partners, with guest messages handled on the AI messenger and you looped in on escalations. You earn 50% of PropertyFlow’s management fee, excluding VAT, on every property you introduce, paid monthly on a self-billed statement. Your own properties go on standard terms with no commission. It is by application and we accept around one in five.
