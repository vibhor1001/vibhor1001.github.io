Frequently asked question

Anything else? Email us at info@propertyflow.uk

What’s included in PropertyFlow’s free plan, and how does pricing work?

Get the full PropertyFlow platform for your first 3 properties free, with no monthly subscription fee and no card required. From your 4th property onwards, pricing starts at £9.99 per additional property, per month.

What happens when I add more than 3 properties?

Once you add your 4th property, you move onto our paid plan, starting at £9.99 per additional property, per month.

What can I manage through PropertyFlow?

PropertyFlow brings your bookings, pricing, guest communication, team operations, reporting and compliance together in one place.

Does PropertyFlow help with compliance?

Yes. You can keep important property compliance information and documents organised and track key renewal dates through the platform.

What is Portfolio Partner and how does it work?

Portfolio Partner is designed to help you build and oversee your property portfolio with the right support behind you. You focus on finding opportunities, building relationships and closing deals, while PropertyFlow provides the technology, one-to-one mentoring and backend support, with day-to-day ground operations coordinated through our service partners.
