Listing management, connected calendars and dynamic pricing are free for life for every user. There is no software subscription for these tools. Booking-channel commissions, PropertyFlow distribution fees and optional fully managed services are separate.

Your management fee is agreed for your property before you sign. Cleaning, linen, maintenance and other property costs are separate from the free software. Ask our team for a proposal that sets out the scope, fees and VAT.

Yes. You can connect supported existing channel accounts, including your own Airbnb and Booking.com accounts. The team can confirm eligibility and the connection process for each listing.

With self-managed hosting, you arrange your own cleaning and property team. With fully managed hosting, the operations team coordinates cleaning, linen and maintenance through service providers, with costs covered by your agreement.

Fully managed owners receive booking and property-activity updates, plus an itemised monthly statement showing income, expenses and deductions. Financial records connect costs and payouts to the relevant stays.

On your own channel accounts, that channel’s payout arrangement applies. For fully managed properties, PropertyFlow pays owners monthly with an itemised statement. Your agreement sets the payout terms and deductions; the team confirms these before you start.
