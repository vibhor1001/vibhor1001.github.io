What does PropertyFlow’s channel manager do?

It runs your booking channels from one calendar and one inbox. Connect the Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo accounts you already have (your channels), or let PropertyFlow list your property on the channels you are not on (PropertyFlow channels). Availability, per-channel rates and guest messages are then managed in one place.

How does PropertyFlow help prevent double-bookings?

When a booking comes through a connected channel, availability is updated across your connected calendars, helping prevent the same dates from being booked twice.

Can I manage multiple booking channels from PropertyFlow?

Yes. PropertyFlow is designed to bring your connected channels together, so you can manage your portfolio from one central system rather than checking each platform individually.

What is the difference between your channels and PropertyFlow channels?

Your channels: you connect your own Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo and other accounts. Free for up to 3 properties. PropertyFlow channels: we list your property on the channels you are not on, for 5% plus VAT of each booking we bring, on top of that channel’s own commission at cost. Bookings through your own Airbnb account: 0%. Available on Free and Premium. Switch back any time.

Is PropertyFlow suitable for smaller portfolios?

Yes. Whether you're starting with one property or managing a growing portfolio, PropertyFlow is designed to give you one place to manage your day-to-day operations.

What’s the difference between iCal and API connections?

iCal primarily synchronises calendar availability between platforms. API connections can support a deeper exchange of information, depending on what each booking channel makes available through its integration.
