How it works

Everything you need.
All in one place.

The moment a guest books anywhere, PropertyFlow closes those dates on your other channels. API-connected channels update in real time; iCal connections refresh on each platform's schedule. No manual step.

Bring guest conversations from connected booking channels into one workspace, so your team can manage messages without jumping between platforms.
