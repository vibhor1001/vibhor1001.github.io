02Three things you stop doing

Nobody builds a statement. Nobody re-types a payout.

Every payout, expense and statement tied to the stay it belongs to.

01

Owner statements

Each property gets its own owner statement, built from the bookings and costs that actually happened. Never a shared template with the wrong numbers in it.

02

Payout tracking

Every channel payout is logged against the stays it covers, so you can see what has been paid, what is still pending and what each booking actually netted.

03

Expenses tied to stays

A cleaning bill or a channel fee is attached to the stay that caused it, instead of sitting in a folder waiting to be matched up by hand.

03Every payout in one place

Every payout and cost, in one place.

Channel payouts and supplier costs sit alongside the bookings they belong to, so your records are easy to check, understand and keep up to date.
