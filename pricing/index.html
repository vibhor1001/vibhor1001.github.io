Start free and manage your property yourself. Or choose fully managed hosting and let our team handle the work behind every stay.

01The Partner Programme

Introduce property owners. Build a business with us.

The Partner Programme is not a hosting plan. It is for people who introduce property owners to PropertyFlow, or who want to work with us. You introduce landlords and hold the relationship. PropertyFlow contracts with the landlord, runs cleaning, linen, maintenance and licensing through the back office, and pays you 50% of PropertyFlow's management fee, excluding VAT, on every property you introduce, on a monthly self-billed statement. By application. We accept around one in five.
