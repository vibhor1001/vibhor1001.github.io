1. Who we are

PropertyFlow Technologies Ltd (PropertyFlow, we, us) is the controller of the personal data described in this notice, except where this notice says that we act as a processor for a Host. We are registered in England and Wales, company number 16927756, registered office 20-22 Wenlock Road, London, N1 7GU, and with the Information Commissioner's Office under number ZC088419. Contact us about privacy at info@propertyflow.uk with 'Privacy' in the subject line.

Where a property is managed under the PropertyFlow Partner Programme or the Own a property service, the contracting company is our group company PropertyFlow Management Partners Ltd, company number 17335928, same registered office. The two companies share systems and this notice covers both. Where both companies decide how data is used, they are joint controllers and PropertyFlow Technologies Ltd is the point of contact.

2. Who this notice covers

  • Hosts: people and businesses with a PropertyFlow account, their team members, cleaners and property owners.
  • Guests: people who book or stay at a property listed or managed through PropertyFlow.
  • Partners and landlords: applicants to and members of the Partner Programme, and property owners who enquire about or use the Own a property service.
  • Website and app visitors: anyone who visits propertyflow.uk, uses the apps, contacts us or receives our marketing.

3. Our role

For Hosts, Partners, landlords and website visitors we are the controller. For Guest data on a Host's own channels we are the Host's processor and act on the Host's instructions under Schedule 2 of the Platform Terms; the Host is the controller and Guests should contact the Host first. For Guest data on PropertyFlow channels, where bookings are taken through PropertyFlow's own channel accounts, we are a controller together with the Host and the channel. For managed properties, PropertyFlow Management Partners Ltd is the controller for the landlord relationship and for Guest stays.

4. What we collect

Hosts, Partners and landlords

  • Identity and contact details: name, email, phone, postal address, business name and company number.
  • Account and property details: login details, property addresses, descriptions, photographs, house rules, licence and certificate documents and expiry dates, team members and their roles.
  • Booking and financial data: bookings, rates, occupancy, revenue, statements, payouts, bank account details, invoices and payment history. Card details are held by our payment processor, not by us.
  • Tax information for HMRC platform reporting where you use PropertyFlow channels: date of birth, National Insurance number or Unique Taxpayer Reference, company number, VAT number and property addresses.
  • Partner Programme data: application details, experience, references, agreement and commission records.
  • Communications: emails, messages in the platform, call notes, support requests and demo bookings.
  • Usage data: device, IP address, app version, pages and features used, crash reports and log data.

Guests

  • Name, email, phone number and country as passed to us by the channel or entered by the Host.
  • Booking details: property, dates, number of guests, price, payment status, special requests and check-in information.
  • Messages exchanged with the Host through the platform, including drafts produced by AI messaging.
  • Identity verification, where a channel provides it, and any damage claim records.
  • Reviews and ratings.

Website and app visitors

  • Contact form, demo booking, Partner application and Own a property report requests, including the property address, bedrooms and property standard you give us.
  • Cookie and analytics data described in the Cookie Policy.
  • Marketing preferences.

5. Where the data comes from

From you, when you create an account, add properties, apply to the Partner Programme, request a report or contact us. From booking channels (the Airbnb and Booking.com accounts you connect, and Vrbo, Agoda, Trip.com, Hopper, Google Vacation Rentals and other PropertyFlow channels) through our connectivity provider, Channex. From our pricing data provider, PriceLabs, for market rates and occupancy. From our payment processor. From public sources such as Companies House where we verify a business. From our website and apps through cookies and analytics.

7. AI features

AI Cognito Pricing uses booking history, market data and property information to suggest nightly rates. AI messaging uses property information and message history to draft replies to Guests. Both run on our own AI models hosted on Microsoft Azure in the United Kingdom and on Anthropic’s Claude API (United States) under contract with us. Content sent to those providers is used only to produce the output for the Host concerned and is not used to train models made available to others. Neither feature makes decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on Guests or Hosts: rates are suggestions the Host controls, and messages are drafts the Host can review or switch off.

8. Who we share it with

We share personal data only as needed to run the platform and meet our obligations, with:

  • Booking channels you connect or that we list you on, to publish listings and take bookings.
  • Our sub-processors: Channex (channel connectivity), PriceLabs (pricing and market data), Stripe (payments, United Kingdom), Microsoft Azure including Application Insights (hosting and performance monitoring, United Kingdom), Sentry (error monitoring, United States), Xero (accounting and bank reconciliation, United States), Trustpilot (review invitations after direct bookings, Denmark), Brevo with Resend as fallback (transactional email, France and United States), our own AI models on Microsoft Azure (United Kingdom) and Anthropic (Claude API, United States) for AI Cognito Pricing and AI messaging, HubSpot (CRM, forms and support), GitHub (website hosting), Google (Analytics, Ads measurement, Search Console and business tools) and Meta and LinkedIn (advertising measurement, only with your consent). The current list is at propertyflow.uk/subprocessors.
  • Our group company PropertyFlow Management Partners Ltd, for managed properties and the Partner Programme, and Partners in the Partner Programme, who receive the details of landlords and property enquiries routed to them for their area.
  • Our staff and contractors, including our team in India, under confidentiality and access controls.
  • HM Revenue and Customs, for platform reporting, and councils, for tourist tax where the law requires.
  • Professional advisers, insurers, banks and auditors, where necessary.
  • Courts, regulators and law enforcement where required by law, and a buyer of our business if we sell it, on notice to you.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share Guest data with advertisers.

9. International transfers

Our team includes staff and contractors in India, and some of our providers are in the United States. Where personal data leaves the UK we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or on the UK-US Data Bridge where a US provider is certified under it, and we keep a transfer risk assessment on file. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards at the contact address above.

11. Security

We protect personal data with encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access, multi-factor authentication for staff, logging and monitoring, regular backups, supplier due diligence and a tested incident response process. No system is completely secure; if a breach affects you we will tell you and, where required, the Information Commissioner's Office within 72 hours of becoming aware.

12. Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. To exercise a right, email info@propertyflow.uk with 'Privacy' in the subject line. We respond within one month. If you are a Guest and your data is held on a Host's own channels, we will pass your request to the Host and help them respond.

13. Cookies

Our Cookie Policy explains the cookies and similar technologies we use on the website and in the apps, and how to manage them.

14. Children

The platform is for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from children except Guest details a Host or channel gives us about people in a booking party, which we use only to run the stay.

15. Changes to this notice

We will post any change here and, if it is significant, tell Hosts by email and in the platform at least 30 days before it takes effect.

16. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, contact us first at info@propertyflow.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
