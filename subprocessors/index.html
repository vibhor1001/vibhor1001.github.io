Effective from 10 September 2026. Version 1.0.

About this list

PropertyFlow Technologies Ltd uses the third-party providers below (sub-processors) to run the PropertyFlow platform, website and apps. This is the list referred to in section 8 of the Privacy Notice and in Schedule 2 of the Platform Terms. Each provider is bound by a contract that limits its use of personal data to the purpose shown.

We give Hosts 30 days' notice of any new sub-processor by email or in the platform (Platform Terms, clause S2.5). Where a provider is outside the UK, transfers are made under the safeguards described in section 9 of the Privacy Notice.
